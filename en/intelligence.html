Our Framework

Investment Decision Framework

We don't predict the future; we analyze current data, market realities and risks.

Location Quality

Micro-district dynamics and social infrastructure adequacy.

Transport Networks

Access to main arteries and planned infrastructure projects.

Liquidity

Average sale velocity in the district and secondary-market depth.

Rental Market

Rental yield multiplier ratios and institutional tenant demand.

Growth Potential

Zoning plan changes and investments shifting toward the district.

Urban Transformation

Age profile of the existing building stock and transformation feasibility.

Buyer Profile

Demographic makeup and demand continuity for the district.

Legal Review

Title deed encumbrances, zoning status and legal pre-screening.

GDOS Market Principles

No Invented Statistics

No data is shared unless it rests on objective sources.

Verified Information First

We base our analysis on recorded title deed and rental data — not market rumors.

No Unrealistic Promises

Returns above market reality are never used as a marketing tool.

Strategy Before Property

The investor's timeline, risk appetite and goals are analyzed before any portfolio is presented.

No Speculative Forecasting

We speak about the analysis of current data, not predictions about future prices.

Data Before Opinion

Mathematical ratios (such as rental yield multiplier) take priority over personal opinion.
