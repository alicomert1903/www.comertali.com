Before recommending a property, we answer three questions: Which direction is rental demand heading in this area? How many times inflation is the value appreciation? Does urban transformation risk justify this price? No offer reaches the table without answering all three.

Region-Based Market Analysis

Every offer is assessed on gross rental multiplier, CPI-deflated value appreciation and average vacancy period. These dynamics, which vary district by district in Ankara, form the foundation of the investment decision.
